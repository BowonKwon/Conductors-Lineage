Conductors' lineage

Who taught each conductor, and under whom did each one work as an assistant? Every relation names its source and, wherever possible, carries the sentence that states it.

What this page shows Conductors and their teachers are placed by year of birth, with arrows showing who studied with whom and who worked under whom as an assistant conductor.

What you can see The site holds 854 relations among 1,010 people (572 on the list and 438 outside it who appear in a relation, 436 of them only as a teacher): formal study 651, assistant 92, short study 111. 826 are confirmed from sources other than Wikipedia; for 28 the sources disagree.

Take care No line does not mean no relation. The 265 candidate source rows, found only in Wikipedia or in a source that was not opened directly, are not drawn. This is exploratory edition 0.1.1; independent verification is not finished.
