Orchestra Festival

What this page shows For each year of the Orchestra Festival at the Seoul Arts Center, this page lists which orchestra appeared under which conductor.

What you can see 635 concerts are collected from the 37 years, out of the 38 from 1989 to 2026, for which records were found. Of 189 conductors, 103 are on this site's list. The year with the most concerts is 1995 (25); the fewest is 2004 (9).

Take care Only the 98 concerts from 2022 on were checked against the Seoul Arts Center's concert pages. The 537 earlier ones come from a list in the Korean Wikipedia and are candidates. No record was found for 2021.

The Orchestra Festival (교향악축제) is held by the Seoul Arts Center (the records gathered here begin in 1989); orchestras from across Korea take the stage in turn. This site used the festival's appearance records to build its list of Korean conductors and publishes those records here. A conductor whose name is a link (the 103 who are on this site's list) leads to that person's lineage tree.

Appearances year by year#

The Wikipedia list was compared with 29 rows taken separately from newspaper articles (1989, 1994, 1997, 1999, 2019). Of the 12 rows with a date, 12 have a concert on the same day in the Wikipedia list, and of the 11 of those that name a conductor, the conductor agrees in 11. For the 17 rows without a date, only the presence of the same conductor in that year's list was checked: 15 are present and 2 are not. Differences in spacing, and the two spellings of one conductor's name (임평용, 임평룡), were treated as agreement. Orchestra names are literal translations.
