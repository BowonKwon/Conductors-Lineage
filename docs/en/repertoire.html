Festival repertoire

What this page shows This page counts the repertoire of the Orchestra Festival: which works by which composers were programmed.

What you can see 255 works were programmed on the 97 concerts, out of 98 in 2022–2026, for which a program was obtained. The composer programmed most often is Beethoven (23 works), followed by Shostakovich (17) and Tchaikovsky (17). Concertos account for 95 works, 37% of the total.

Take care The programs are those printed on the Seoul Arts Center's concert pages. The pages say that a program may change, so a program may differ from what was played. No program records were found for the years before 2022, and five years are too few to speak of a trend.

Of the appearances on the Orchestra Festival page, 98 concerts in 2022–2026 were checked on the Seoul Arts Center's site. The works were taken from the program section of those pages; a program was obtained for 97 concerts (no concert page was found for the concert of Ulsan Municipal Symphony Orchestra on 2026-04-23, which is left out). A concert had 2.6 works on average. The table gives the number of concerts and works year by year.

The subscription-concert programs of four Korean orchestras (Seoul, Suwon, Bucheon and Busan) are in section 7 of the orchestra comparison page of Classical Music Metrics.

Programs year by year#

The figures beside each year are concerts with a program out of that year's concerts, and the number of works. A conductor's name that is a link leads to the Find a person page; a name marked * is a romanization made for this site (see Known limits). Titles that could not be put into English are left in Korean. A remark in parentheses after a title, such as a version or an editor's name, marks the edition of the score. In the data file, abbreviations with a number at the end of a title, such as Op., K. or Sz., are numbers in the catalog of a composer's works.

How the works were collected and counted#

The concert pages on the Seoul Arts Center's site (one page per concert) were opened and the text of their program sections was copied. The paths the site closes to automated collection (search and file attachments) were not opened. For concerts before 2022 no concert pages were found on the site, so no programs were obtained.

Composers' names are spelled differently from year to year on the concert pages and were brought to one spelling. Where a page gives composer and work on one line without a separator, the line was split with a list of composers' names. English titles appear only on the pages of 2022 and 2023, so the English titles here follow a rule for numbered symphonies and concertos and the customary English title for other works.

The data are in festival_works.csv. It keeps the line as printed on the concert page, so composers and kinds can be sorted again.
