Conductors' lineage

Who taught each conductor, and under whom did each one work as an assistant? Every relation names its source and, wherever possible, carries the sentence that states it.

What this page shows Conductors and their teachers are placed by year of birth, with arrows showing who studied with whom and who worked under whom as an assistant conductor.

What you can see The site holds 952 relations among 1,058 people (572 on the list and 486 outside it who appear in a relation, 483 of them only as a teacher): formal study 705, assistant 108, short study 139. 913 are confirmed from sources other than Wikipedia; for 39 the sources disagree.

Take care No line does not mean no relation. The 153 candidate source rows, found only in Wikipedia or Wikidata or not confirmed in another source, are not drawn. This is exploratory edition 0.2.1; independent verification is not finished.
